Course Overview

Central to the theory of computation are the concepts of automata, formal languages, grammar, algorithms, computability, decidability, and complexity. Parts of the theory have direct bearing on practice, such as Automata on circuit design, compiler design, and search algorithms; Formal Languages and Grammars on compiler design; and Complexity on cryptography and optimization problems.

Learning Outcomes

  • Introduce students to the mathematical foundations of computation including automata theory, grammars, algorithms, decidability, complexity, and computability.
  • Enhance students' ability to understand and conduct mathematical proofs for computation and algorithms.

Textbooks

  • Required: M. Sipser, Introduction to the Theory of Computation, 2nd Edition
  • Required: Hopcroft, Motwani, Ullman, Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages and Computation, 3rd Edition
